Accountant Mode Guide

Automatic P&L, Financial Score and tax estimates straight from your cash register.

What is Accountant Mode

Accountant Mode turns your Daily Cash Register entries into ready-to-use accounting reports: P&L, Financial Score and MEI tax estimate. You speak the same language as your accountant, with reliable numbers.

Who it is for: any retailer who wants to truly understand how much the store makes, without building a spreadsheet.

Automatic P&L

The P&L sorts transactions into four groups:

  • Revenue: everything tagged as Sale.
  • COGS: Replenishment and raw materials.
  • Operating expenses: Packaging, Shipping, Other.
  • Owner pay: withdrawals flagged as Personal.

The system automatically calculates Gross Profit, EBITDA, Net Profit and Real Margin for the month.

Financial Score

The Score (0 to 100) shows your store health in four pillars:

  1. Margin: real profit as a percentage of revenue.
  2. Controlled owner pay: personal withdrawals under 30% of revenue.
  3. Diversification: not relying on a single product or customer.
  4. Consistency: steady monthly revenue.

Taxes (MEI)

Accountant Mode estimates your fixed monthly DAS and warns you if the annual projection exceeds the R$ 81,000 MEI limit. No nasty surprises from the tax authority.

Best practices

  • Log every transaction in the Daily Cash Register without exception.
  • Use the correct categories so the P&L stays accurate.
  • Flag personal withdrawals with the Personal toggle.
  • Export the monthly PDF and send it to your accountant.
