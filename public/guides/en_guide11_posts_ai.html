PostsAI for Instagram Guide

Generate professional Instagram posts for your products using artificial intelligence.

What is PostsAI

PostsAI uses artificial intelligence (Google Gemini) to generate, in seconds, a complete Instagram post for your product: a 1:1 feed-style image, persuasive caption and optimized hashtags.

When to use it

  • Launching a new product.
  • Flash promotion or discount.
  • Reactivating a product that was stagnant.
  • When you simply have no idea what to post.

How to generate a post

  1. Go to Storefront and pick the product.
  2. Tap the "Post AI" button.
  3. Choose the tone: Promotional, Elegant or Playful.
  4. Tap "Generate post" and wait a few seconds.
  5. Download the image and copy the caption.
Tip: invest in a good original product photo. The AI uses it as reference. Good photo in, amazing post out.

Available tones

  • Promotional: highlights price, discount and urgency.
  • Elegant: sophisticated tone focused on quality and differentials.
  • Playful: light language, emojis and a relaxed CTA.

Limits and best practices

  • 1 post per minute to keep costs sane.
  • Always review the caption before publishing.
  • Set your WhatsApp number and store name in Settings so the CTA renders correctly.
  • Combine with Stories and Reels to multiply reach.
Important: the AI generates content from what you have registered. The better the product name, description and photo, the better the result.
