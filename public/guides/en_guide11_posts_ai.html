What is PostsAI

PostsAI uses artificial intelligence (Google Gemini) to generate, in seconds, a complete Instagram post for your product: a 1:1 feed-style image, a persuasive caption, and optimized hashtags.

No design skills needed, no Canva, no agency. Pick the product, pick the tone, tap generate. Done.

When to use it

  • Launching a new product.
  • Flash promotion or one-time discount.
  • Reactivating a product that has been sitting in your storefront.
  • When you simply have no idea what to post.

How to generate a post

  1. Go to Storefront and choose the product you want to promote.
  2. Tap the "Post AI" button on the product card.
  3. Choose the tone: Promotional, Elegant, or Playful.
  4. Tap "Generate post" and wait a few seconds.
  5. Download the image and copy the ready-to-publish caption.
Tip: invest in a good original product photo. The AI uses it as a reference to compose the generated image. Good photo in, amazing post out.

Available tones

Tone 1

Promotional

Highlights price, discount, and urgency. Ideal for clearance sales, flash deals, and launches with a special offer.

Generated caption example: "Today only! Women's shoes at 20% OFF. Grab yours before they're gone. Message us on WhatsApp and lock in your order now."
Tone 2

Elegant

Sophisticated tone focused on product differentials and quality. Works best for premium products, fashion, accessories, and higher-ticket items.

Generated caption example: "Crafted for those who appreciate the details. Handmade footwear, genuine leather, comfort you feel from the very first step. Check availability."
Tone 3

Playful

Light language, emojis, and a relaxed CTA. Perfect for everyday products, younger audiences, and brands with a more informal personality.

Generated caption example: "Does your foot deserve nice things? Because this shoe says yes. Next-level comfort. Send us a message and let's make it happen."

Limits and best practices

  • Limit of 1 post per minute to keep costs under control.
  • Always review the caption before publishing on Instagram.
  • Set your WhatsApp number and store name in Settings so the CTA renders correctly.
  • Combine generated posts with Stories and Reels to multiply your reach.
  • Use PostsAI consistently: a regular feed builds authority and trust.
  • Test all three tones and track which one drives more engagement for your audience.
Important: the AI generates content based on what you have registered. The better the product name, description, and photo, the better the output. A poorly filled product generates a weak post.

What influences output quality

PostsAI is only as good as what you feed it. Three factors make all the difference:

Factor 1

Product photo

The generated image is composed from your original photo. A clean background, good lighting, and a centered product result in far more professional posts.

Factor 2

Product name

A clear and descriptive name helps the AI understand what is being sold. "Women's Comfort Beige Shoe Size 8" produces better results than "Product 01".

Factor 3

Registered description

The more detail you include about materials, differentials, and target audience, the more relevant and personalized the generated caption will be.

Final takeaway: PostsAI does not replace strategy, but it removes the creation barrier. Use it to keep your feed active, test different voices, and scale your Instagram presence without depending on an agency.
