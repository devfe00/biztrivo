Smart MEI Guide

Control your annual revenue cap, monthly DAS and avoid losing your MEI status.

Why monitor your MEI

In 2026 the MEI annual revenue cap is R$ 81,000. Cross it and you are deregistered, become a regular ME and pay much more tax. The Biztrivo MEI module warns you before the problem hits.

Annual cap

The system sums all revenue for the current year and shows:

  • How much you have already invoiced.
  • How much is left until the cap.
  • Year-end projection based on your current pace.
  • Risk alert if the projection exceeds R$ 81,000.
Heads up: exceeding by up to 20% deregisters you the following year with retroactive tax charges. Exceeding by more than 20% deregisters you immediately. Pay attention to alerts.

Monthly DAS

The DAS is the single tax slip combining INSS, ICMS and ISS for MEI. In 2026 the value is R$ 76.90 for commerce. The app reminds you every month, on the 20th, about the due date.

When to move to ME

If you are constantly near the cap, it might be time to plan a migration to Microempresa. Talk to an accountant in advance to avoid trouble at the annual filing.

Monthly checklist

  • Check total invoiced in the month.
  • Pay the DAS by the 20th.
  • Look at the annual projection.
  • Keep revenue receipts for 5 years.
