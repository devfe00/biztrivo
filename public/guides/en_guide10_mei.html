Why monitoring your MEI status matters

In 2026, the annual revenue cap for MEI is R$ 81,000. Crossing that threshold does not just trigger a fine — you are automatically deregistered, reclassified as a Microempresa (ME), and start paying significantly more in taxes, including retroactive charges for the entire year.

Biztrivo's MEI module monitors your revenue in real time and sends alerts before you get close to the limit. It is not about paperwork; it is about protecting what you built.

Heads up: Exceeding the cap by up to 20% triggers deregistration in the following year with retroactive tax charges. Exceeding it by more than 20% triggers immediate deregistration in the same fiscal year. Do not ignore system alerts.

How the annual cap works in practice

Biztrivo totals all revenue recorded since January 1st and shows four essential indicators in real time:

Indicator 1

Total revenue billed this year

The sum of every sale logged in the system from January 1st through today. Every new entry automatically updates this figure — no manual tallying required.

Example: It is July and you have already invoiced R$ 52,000. The dashboard updates that number instantly every time you record a sale.
Indicator 2

How much headroom you have left

The difference between R$ 81,000 and your total invoiced so far. This number is displayed prominently so you always know exactly how much room remains before the cap.

Example: With R$ 52,000 billed, the system shows "R$ 29,000 remaining." Clear and immediate.
Indicator 3

Year-end projection

Based on your average monthly revenue, the system calculates how much you are likely to invoice by December 31st. This lets you plan ahead instead of reacting in a panic.

Example: Your monthly average is R$ 7,500. In July the system projects: R$ 52,000 already billed + R$ 37,500 remaining in the year = R$ 89,500 projected. That is R$ 8,500 over the cap — alert triggered.
Indicator 4

Risk alert

When the year-end projection crosses R$ 81,000, the system displays a visible alert on your dashboard. You get the notification with enough lead time to make a decision: slow down sales in the remaining months, or start planning the migration to ME.

Example: In October, the alert reads: "Your current projection is R$ 89,500. You may exceed the cap in November. Consider speaking with an accountant."

Monthly DAS: what it is and how it works

The DAS (Documento de Arrecadacao do Simples) is the single tax slip that consolidates INSS, ICMS, and ISS into one monthly payment. In 2026, the amount for retail businesses is R$ 76.90 per month.

Paying the DAS on time preserves your social security benefits — retirement, sick pay, maternity pay — and avoids late fees and penalties. The due date is the 20th of every month.

Component What it covers Amount (2026)
INSS Social security (retirement, benefits) R$ 72.90
ICMS Sales tax on goods (retail) R$ 1.00
ISS Service tax (service providers) R$ 5.00
Retail total INSS + ICMS R$ 76.90
Automatic reminder: Biztrivo sends you a notification on the 20th of every month reminding you the DAS is due. You do not need to track any date manually — the system handles it.

When to migrate to Microempresa (ME)

If you are consistently close to the annual cap or have already exceeded it in a given month, it may be time to plan a migration to ME. This is not a setback — it is growth. But it requires planning.

Signal 1

Projection above R$ 75,000 for two consecutive months

If the system projects you will exceed the cap for two months in a row, schedule a meeting with an accountant. Do not wait for deregistration to happen.

Signal 2

You need to hire more than one employee

MEI allows only one registered employee. If your business has grown and you need additional staff, migration to ME or EPP may be necessary regardless of your revenue level.

Signal 3

Your activity is no longer listed under MEI

Not all economic activities are permitted under MEI. If you have diversified your products or services, verify that your activity is still on the authorized list. Biztrivo displays a warning if it detects a mismatch.

Important: Migrating to ME changes your tax regime, monthly obligations, and benefits. Never make this decision alone. Consult an accountant before any change, ideally 3 to 6 months in advance.

Monthly MEI checklist

Set aside 15 minutes a month to go through these items. That is all it takes to keep your MEI status healthy and avoid surprises at year end.

  • Check your total revenue for the month and compare it against your expected pace.
  • Pay the DAS by the 20th (use Biztrivo's automatic reminder).
  • Review the year-end projection and check whether a risk alert is active.
  • Save the month's revenue receipts in an organized digital folder.
  • Confirm that every sale from the month has been correctly logged in the system.
  • Verify that your business activity is still compatible with the MEI regime.

Frequently asked questions

Question 1

What happens if I miss three consecutive DAS payments?

After three months of non-payment, the Brazilian Federal Revenue Service may cancel your MEI CNPJ automatically. You also lose the social security benefits for the months you did not pay. Regularize as soon as possible, the Biztrivo alerts you if you fall behind.

Question 2

Can I pay overdue DAS in installments?

Yes. MEI debts can be paid in installments through the Simples Nacional portal. The minimum installment amount is R$ 50.00. Contact the Federal Revenue Service or an accountant to formalize the arrangement.

Question 3

Do I need to issue invoices as a MEI?

It depends on your customer. For individuals (pessoas fisicas), it is generally not required. For companies (pessoas juridicas), issuing a nota fiscal is mandatory in most cases. Biztrivo offers integrated invoice issuance to keep you compliant with no extra effort.
